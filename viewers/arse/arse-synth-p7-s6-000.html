From rational PD groups to closed manifolds via surgery

synth-p7-s6-000  |  algebraic-topology manifolds homotopy-theory  |  13 nodes, 20 edges

Question

Let $\Gamma$ be a finitely presented group whose cohomology satisfies rational Poincar\'e duality of dimension $n$. The proof sketch claims that once $\Gamma$ embeds as the fundamental group of an $FH(\mathbb Q)$ complex and the relevant surgery obstruction disappears, one can realize $\Gamma$ as the $\pi_1$ of a closed $n$-manifold. What are the precise obstruction groups that appear in this composite process? In particular, how do Wall's finiteness obstruction, the rational Poincar\'e complex produced from $\Gamma$, and the $L_n(\mathbb Z[\Gamma])$ surgery obstruction fit together to yield an actual manifold?

Answer

There are three layers. First one needs a finite complex that has $\Gamma$ as its fundamental group. Wall's finiteness obstruction is the class

$$\omega(\Gamma)\in K_0(\mathbb Z[\Gamma])\cong \\widetilde K_0(\mathbb Z[\Gamma])\oplus\mathbb Z,$$

which vanishes if and only if the universal cover of the classifying space has a finite $\mathbb Z[\Gamma]$-resolution. The 2-torsion lattices considered here are of type $F$, so $\omega(\Gamma)=0$ and we obtain a finite CW-complex $X$ with $\pi_1(X)=\Gamma$. The cohomological assumption gives cap-product isomorphisms $H^i(X;\mathbb Q)\\cong H_{n-i}(X;\mathbb Q)$; thus $X$ is an $FH(\mathbb Q)$-complex, i.e. a rational Poincar\'e complex.

Second, a degree-one normal map $f:M^n\\rightarrow X$ is constructed by thickening the $n$-skeleton of $X$ and stabilizing the normal bundle. Its normal invariant $\eta(f)\\in[X,G/TOP]$ records whether $f$ is normally cobordant to a homotopy equivalence. This class maps to Wall's surgery obstruction

$$\theta(f)\\in L_n(\mathbb Z[\Gamma]),$$

via the surgery exact sequence $0\\to S^{TOP}(X)\\to[X,G/TOP]\\xrightarrow{\theta}L_n(\mathbb Z[\Gamma])$. The third layer is therefore purely algebraic: compute $L_n(\mathbb Z[\Gamma])$ for the chosen lattice. In the 2-torsion cases the assembly map shows that $L_n(\mathbb Z[\Gamma])$ is torsion and the specific class $\theta(f)$ vanishes after arranging the required characteristic classes.

When $\theta(f)=0$, the normal invariant lifts to the structure set $S^{TOP}(X)$, and we obtain a closed manifold $M$ together with a simple homotopy equivalence $M\\to X$ inducing an isomorphism on $\pi_1$. Summarizing: $\omega(\Gamma)=0$ ensures finiteness ($FH(\mathbb Q)$), the rational Poincar\'e duality of $\Gamma$ identifies the homotopy type to which surgery is applied, and the vanishing of $\theta(f)$ in $L_n(\mathbb Z[\Gamma])$ removes the final obstruction. Under these conditions $\Gamma$ is realized as $\pi_1$ of a closed manifold obtained by composing finiteness theory and surgery.
