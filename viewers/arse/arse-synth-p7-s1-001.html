Fixed-point strata of torsion lattices on symmetric spaces

synth-p7-s1-001  |  lie-groups orbifolds differential-topology  |  13 nodes, 20 edges

Question

Suppose $\Gamma< G$ is a uniform lattice in a semisimple Lie group whose action on $X=G/K$ contains an involution $g$. How can we describe the fixed-point set $X^g$ and the resulting singular stratum of the orbifold $X/\Gamma$, and why does passing to a torsion-free finite-index subgroup remove these singularities?

Answer

Cartan's fixed-point theorem shows that an isometry of finite order on the nonpositively curved symmetric space $X$ fixes a totally geodesic submanifold $X^g$. Because the stabilizer $\Gamma_x$ of any $x\in X^g$ is finite, the local model of $X^g$ near $x$ is just the fixed-point subspace of the isotropy representation of $\Gamma_x$ on $T_xX$. The normal directions transform by a nontrivial representation of $\Gamma_x$, so the quotient looks like $(T_xX^{g} \oplus N_x)/\Gamma_x$, i.e. an orbifold chart with a mirror (if $g$ acts as a reflection) or higher-order cone.

Selberg's lemma provides a torsion-free subgroup $\Gamma'\subset \Gamma$ of finite index. Because torsion elements are exactly those creating nontrivial stabilizers, the action of $\Gamma'$ on $X$ is free, so $X/\Gamma'$ is a genuine manifold and the covering map $X/\Gamma' \to X/\Gamma$ ramifies precisely over the singular strata determined by the various $X^g$. Thus the singularities in $X/\Gamma$ disappear after removing torsion, confirming that orbifold issues are solely caused by the finite stabilizers coming from torsion elements.

Expressions (3)

\(X^{g}=\{x\in X: gx=x\}\)
\(\Gamma_x=\operatorname{Stab}_\Gamma(x) ext{ is finite}\)
\((T_xX^{g} \oplus N_x)/\Gamma_x ext{ is an orbifold chart}\)
