How does Bredon cohomology give rational Poincaré duality for torsion lattices?

synth-p7-s2-000  |  group-cohomology homological-algebra algebraic-topology  |  13 nodes, 19 edges

Question

Let $X=G/K$ be the symmetric space of a connected semisimple Lie group with finite center and let $\Gamma< G$ be a uniform lattice that may have torsion. Ordinary group cohomology with compact supports gives Poincaré duality for torsion-free lattices, but what should replace it when $\Gamma$ has finite stabilizers? More concretely, how do the Bredon/orbifold cohomology groups $\widehat H^*_c(X/\Gamma;\mathbb Q)$ furnish a rational Poincaré duality pairing of formal dimension $d=\dim X$ for $\Gamma$?

Answer

Because the $\Gamma$-action on $X$ is proper and cocompact, each isotropy group $\Gamma_x$ is finite. One therefore forms the Bredon cohomology of $\Gamma$ with coefficients in the rational Mackey functor given by the trivial representation:

$$\qquad H^*_{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q)=H^*(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O\Gamma}(C_*^{\mathcal F}(X),\underline{\mathbb Q})).$$

The equivariant Eckmann–Shapiro spectral sequence collapses to give

$$\qquad H^*_{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q)\cong H^*_c(X;\mathbb Q),$$

because every fixed-point set $X^{H}$ of a finite subgroup $H\subset\Gamma$ is contractible and carries the same compactly supported cohomology as $X$. Poincaré duality holds for the orientable manifold $X$ of dimension $d$, so cap product with the fundamental class $[X]\in H_d^c(X;\mathbb Q)$ furnishes isomorphisms

$$\qquad H^i_{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q)\cong H_{d-i}^{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q).$$

This is the rational orbifold Poincaré duality satisfied by the orbifold $X/\Gamma$. When $\Gamma$ is torsion-free the Bredon groups reduce to ordinary cohomology, recovering classical duality. Thus the Bredon/orbifold description supplies the desired rational PD structure of formal dimension $d$ even in the presence of torsion.

Expressions (3)

\(H^*_{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O\Gamma}(C_*^{\mathcal F}(X),\underline{\mathbb Q})\)
\(H^*_{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q)\cong H^*_c(X;\mathbb Q)\)
\(H^i_{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q)\cong H_{d-i}^{\mathcal F}(\Gamma;\mathbb Q)\)
