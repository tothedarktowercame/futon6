Why Smith theory imposes no rational obstruction for free Z/2-actions

synth-p7-s5-000  |  algebraic-topology group-theory homology-cohomology  |  13 nodes, 20 edges

Question

Let $X$ be a finite CW-complex that is rationally acyclic ($H_i(X;\mathbb Q)=0$ for $i>0$) and let $\tau$ act cellularly with $\tau^2=1$. Classical Smith theory says that the fixed set $X^{\langle\tau\rangle}$ controls $H_*(X;\mathbb Z/2)$, and many texts conclude that $\mathbb Z/2$-actions on mod-2 acyclic complexes have fixed points. Why does this argument fail over $\mathbb Q$? More concretely, how do transfer and averaging show that a free $\mathbb Z/2$-action on a rationally acyclic space produces no obstruction, even though the mod-2 Smith inequalities still hold?

Answer

Smith theory is inherently $p$-local: the Smith chains are the subcomplexes of the cellular chain complex $C_*(X;\mathbb F_p)$ obtained by restricting and averaging with respect to $\tau$. When the coefficient field has characteristic $p$, the operator $1+\tau$ is nilpotent, so one obtains a long exact sequence relating $H_*(X;\mathbb F_p)$ to $H_*(X^{\langle\tau\rangle};\mathbb F_p)$, forcing nontrivial fixed-point homology whenever $X$ is mod-$p$ acyclic. In characteristic zero, $1+\tau$ is invertible, and the same chain-level argument collapses. Instead we have a transfer map $tr:H_*(X/\tau;\mathbb Q)\to H_*(X;\mathbb Q)$ defined by averaging preimages, and it satisfies $tr\circ\pi_*=2\,id_{H_*(X;\mathbb Q)}$. Because $2$ is invertible in $\mathbb Q$, $\pi_*$ is injective and every class in $H_*(X;\mathbb Q)$ is $2$ times the transfer of a class downstairs. Thus $H_*(X;\mathbb Q)$ carries no information about $X^{\langle\tau\rangle}$.

If the action is free, $\pi:X\to X/\tau$ is a covering of degree $2$ and the transfer shows $H_*(X/\tau;\mathbb Q)\cong H_*(X;\mathbb Q)^\tau$. For a rationally acyclic $X$ this invariant subspace vanishes, so $X/\tau$ is also rationally acyclic and there is no contradiction with Smith theory. The only nontrivial constraints live in mod-2 homology, where $2$ is no longer invertible: there the Smith complex detects fixed-point data through the short exact sequence $0\to C_*(X^{\langle\tau\rangle};\mathbb F_2)\to C_*(X;\mathbb F_2)\xrightarrow{1+\tau} C_*(X;\mathbb F_2)\to 0$. Consequently, mod-2 Smith theory continues to impose fixed-point obstructions, but rational coefficients bypass them entirely because averaging by $1/2$ kills the obstruction.
