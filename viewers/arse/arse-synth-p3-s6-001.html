Proving positivity of F_\mu^*(x;1,t) and P_\lambda^*(x;1,t)

synth-p3-s6-001  |  combinatorics symmetric-functions probability  |  13 nodes, 24 edges

Question

The global smoothing argument in node p3-s6 uses that every stationary weight $\pi(\mu)=F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)/P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)$ is strictly positive for $x_i>0$ and $0\le t<1$. While this is intuitively clear from the combinatorial definition of $F_{\mu}^*$, I would like a precise justification that treats both numerator and denominator. How do the Haglund-Haiman-Loehr tableaux formula and the finite nature of the sector sum imply that $F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)>0$ and $P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)>0$, so that the CTMC stationary law is well defined?

Answer

By definition $F_{\mu}^*(X;q,t)=b_{\mu}(q,t)F_{\mu}[X/(1-t);q,t]$. At $q=1$ the plethystic substitution amounts to replacing each variable with the positive series $x_i(1+t+t^2+\cdots)$, so every monomial coefficient in the Schur-positive expansion becomes a sum of nonnegative terms. The HHL formula then reads

$$F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)=\sum_{T\in\operatorname{SSYT}(\mu)} b_{\mu}(1,t)^{-1} x^{\mathrm{wt}(T)} t^{\mathrm{maj}(T)},$$

and since $x_i>0$ and $0\le t<1$, every term of the sum is positive, proving $F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)>0$.

The normalization constant over the finite sector is

$$P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)=\sum_{ u\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{ u}^*(x;1,t).$$

Each summand is positive by the argument above, and the sum is finite because the sector contains finitely many configurations. Therefore $P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)>0$, so the stationary probabilities $\pi(\mu)=F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)/P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)$ are well defined and strictly positive for every $\mu\in S_n(\lambda)$.

Expressions (3)

\(F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)=\sum_{T\in\operatorname{SSYT}(\mu)} b_{\mu}(1,t)^{-1} x^{\mathrm{wt}(T)} t^{\mathrm{maj}(T)}\)
\(P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)=\sum_{\nu\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{\nu}^*(x;1,t)\)
\(\pi(\mu)=\frac{F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)}{P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)}\)
