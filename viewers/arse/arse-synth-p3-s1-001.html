Deriving the t-PushTASEP clock from the q=1 Pieri rule

synth-p3-s1-001  |  combinatorics probability markov-chains  |  13 nodes, 22 edges

Question

In the same multispecies $t$-PushTASEP setup, suppose a site rings and species $r$ must decide which of the $m$ weaker species clockwise will absorb it before the push cascade. Sources often claim that the selection law is the truncated geometric mass $t^{k-1}/[m]_t$, derived from the Macdonald raising operators, but the intermediate Pieri computation is usually omitted. Could someone show explicitly how the $q=1$ Pieri coefficients compare $F_{\mu}(x;1,t)$ and $F_{\mu^{(k)}}(x;1,t)$, and why their ratio forces the local probabilities to be $t^{k-1}/[m]_t$ so that the global modified Macdonald stationary law remains invariant?

Answer

Let $\mu$ denote the current configuration and let $\mu^{(k)}$ be the state produced when $r$ skips the first $k-1$ weaker species. Adding a box in column $k$ of the recording tableau corresponds to applying the $q=1$ Pieri operator $E_1[X]=\sum_i x_i$ to $F_{\mu}$. The explicit coefficient (Macdonald, Chap. VI, (6.24)) is

$$\qquad F_{\mu^{(k)}}(x;1,t)=\frac{1-t^{m-k+1}}{1-t^{m}}\,t^{k-1}\,F_{\mu}(x;1,t).$$

After cancelling the common hook terms in the stationary ratios we obtain

$$\qquad \frac{\pi(\mu^{(k)})}{\pi(\mu)}=t^{k-1}\frac{1-t}{1-t^{m}},$$

so if we renormalize by the factor $(1-t^{m})/(1-t)=\sum_{k=1}^m t^{k-1}$ we recover the truncated geometric law. Because every push cascade factors into successive Pieri moves, the same ratio holds for each intermediate state, showing that detailed balance with the modified Macdonald measure is satisfied and the CTMC generator preserves probability.

Expressions (3)

\(\mathbf{P}(k)=\frac{t^{k-1}}{[m]_t}\)
\(F_{\mu^{(k)}}(x;1,t)=\frac{1-t^{m-k+1}}{1-t^{m}}\,t^{k-1}F_{\mu}(x;1,t)\)
\(\frac{\pi(\mu^{(k)})}{\pi(\mu)}=t^{k-1}\frac{1-t}{1-t^{m}}\)
