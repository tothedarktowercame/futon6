Checking AMW irreducibility and normalization on the q=1 ring

synth-p3-s4-001  |  probability markov-chains combinatorics  |  13 nodes, 24 edges

Question

Working again in the $q=1$ ring sector $S_n(\lambda)$ with rates $1/x_j$ and $0\le t<1$, the AMW result declares that the stationary measure of the multispecies exclusion chain is $\pi(\eta)=F_{\eta}(x;1,t)/P_{\lambda}(x;1,t)$. To invoke this result we must know that (i) the CTMC is irreducible on $S_n(\lambda)$ and (ii) the normalization constant $P_{\lambda}(x;1,t)=\sum_{ u\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{ u}(x;1,t)$ is finite and strictly positive. Can someone provide a concrete argument for each point using only the $t$-PushTASEP update rules and basic properties of modified Macdonald polynomials?

Answer

(i) Irreducibility follows from the push-cascade mechanics. Given any two configurations $\eta$ and $\xi$, order the particles in $\eta$ lexicographically by species label. Each clock event either swaps an adjacent pair or performs a push that is equivalent to a sequence of adjacent swaps. Because the allowed transitions include every adjacent swap of unequal species with positive rate, we can bubble sort $\eta$ into the canonical sorted word and then reverse the sequence to reach $\xi$. Hence the graph of states connected by positive-rate updates is the Cayley graph of the multiset permutations, so the chain is irreducible.

(ii) Positivity of the sector sum is immediate from Macdonald theory. At $q=1$ the modified Macdonald polynomial $F_{ u}(x;1,t)$ has a nonnegative monomial expansion when $x_i>0$ and $0\le t<1$ (Macdonald, Chap. VI). Therefore each term in

$\qquad P_{\lambda}(x;1,t)=\sum_{ u\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{ u}(x;1,t)$

is strictly positive, and there are only $|S_n(\lambda)|$ terms, so the sum is finite and positive. These two facts justify the hypotheses of the AMW theorem and ensure that $\pi(\eta)=F_{\eta}(x;1,t)/P_{\lambda}(x;1,t)$ is a well-defined stationary distribution for the ring chain.

Expressions (3)

\(\pi(\eta)=\frac{F_{\eta}(x;1,t)}{P_{\lambda}(x;1,t)}\)
\(P_{\lambda}(x;1,t)=\sum_{\nu\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{\nu}(x;1,t)\)
\(F_{\nu}(x;1,t)>0 ext{ whenever } x_i>0, 0\le t<1\)
