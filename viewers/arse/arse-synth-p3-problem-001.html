How do Macdonald star factors interact with the CTMC normalization?

synth-p3-problem-001  |  combinatorics probability symmetric-functions  |  13 nodes, 22 edges

Question

Take a partition $\lambda$ with $\lambda_n=0$ and let $S_n(\lambda)$ be the content-fixed sector used for the modified Macdonald CTMC. The claimed stationary law is $\pi(\mu)=F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)/P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)$, yet the jump rates are written in terms of the unstarred polynomials and truncated geometric normalizations $[m]_t=\frac{1-t^m}{1-t}$. Why is it legitimate to replace every $F_{\mu}^*$ with $F_{\mu}$ divided by the product $b_{\mu}(1,t)=\prod_{s\in\mu}(1-t^{1+\mathrm{leg}(s)})$ and to normalize globally by $P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)=\sum_{ u\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{ u}^*(x;1,t)$? In other words, how do we prove that the star/non-star conversion is purely multiplicative and therefore compatible with the local clock probabilities in the CTMC construction?

Answer

Macdonald's plethystic definition gives $F_{\mu}^*(X;q,t)=b_{\mu}(q,t)F_{\mu}[X/(1-t);q,t]$, so at $q=1$ the only difference between the starred and unstarred specialisations is the scalar

$$b_{\mu}(1,t)=\prod_{s\in\mu}(1-t^{1+\mathrm{leg}(s)})=\prod_{i\ge 1}(t;t)_{m_i(\mu)},$$

where the final equality uses that the legs count repeated parts. Because this factor depends solely on the multiplicities $m_i(\mu)$, every local transition that changes the content by moving one particle multiplies $b_{\mu}(1,t)$ by exactly the same truncated geometric factor that appears in the update rule. Thus the detailed-balance ratio

$$\frac{\pi(\mu)}{\pi( u)}=\frac{F_{\mu}(x;1,t)}{F_{ u}(x;1,t)}\cdot\frac{b_{ u}(1,t)}{b_{\mu}(1,t)}$$

matches the ratio imposed by the local clocks, showing that the CTMC is consistent with the starred stationary law. Summing over the sector gives

$$P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)=\sum_{ u\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{ u}(x;1,t)\,b_{ u}(1,t)^{-1},$$

so the normalization remains a strict positive constant because the sum of Macdonald polynomials over the finite sector is finite. Hence the star/non-star bridge is purely multiplicative: $F_{\mu}^*=F_{\mu}/b_{\mu}(1,t)$, $P_{\lambda}^*=\sum F_{ u}/b_{ u}(1,t)$, and the CTMC local probabilities automatically glue to the global stationary distribution.

Expressions (3)

\(\pi(\mu)=\frac{F_{\mu}^*(x;1,t)}{P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)}\)
\(b_{\mu}(1,t)=\prod_{s\in\mu}(1-t^{1+\operatorname{leg}(s)})\)
\(P_{\lambda}^*(x;1,t)=\sum_{\nu\in S_n(\lambda)}F_{\nu}^*(x;1,t)\)
