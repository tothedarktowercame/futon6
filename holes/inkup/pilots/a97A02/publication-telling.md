# a97A02 publication telling

## Publication

**[B1]** Work with Lebesgue measure restricted to \( [0,1] \): the window
\( [t-h,t+h] \) contributes its part inside the interval, and
\( f_h(t) = (2h)^{-1}\int_{[t-h,t+h]} f \).

**[B3]** The restricted measure of the window is at most \( 2h \).

**[B4]** Let \( F(b) = \int_{(-\infty,b]} f \); since the measure is finite,
so that \( f \in L^{1} \), and atomless, \( F \) and \( t \mapsto F(t\pm h) \)
are continuous on \( [0,1] \).

**[B5]** The numerator is \( F(t+h) - F(t-h) \), so \( f_h \) is continuous
on \( [0,1] \).

**[B6]** Hölder on the window gives
\( \int_{[t-h,t+h]}\lvert f\rvert \le \lVert f\rVert_{p}(2h)^{1-1/p} \).

**[B7]** Therefore
\( \lvert f_h(t)\rvert \le (2h)^{-1/p}\lVert f\rVert_{p} \), the constant being the
dual norm of the normalised window indicator, and the estimate holds for
every real \( t \).

**[B8]** Thus for every \( h > 0 \) and \( 1 \le p < \infty \), \( f_h \) is
continuous on \( [0,1] \) and satisfies the displayed bound.
