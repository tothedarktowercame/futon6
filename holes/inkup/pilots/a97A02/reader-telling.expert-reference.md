# a97A02 reader-oriented telling

## Expert reference

**[B1]** Integrate against \( \mu \), Lebesgue measure restricted to
\( [0,1] \); the window \( [t-h,t+h] \) contributes its part inside the
interval, and \( f_h(t) = (2h)^{-1}\int_{[t-h,t+h]} f\,d\mu \).

**[B3]** The restricted measure of the window is at most \( 2h \), for every
\( t \).

**[B4]** Let \( F(b) = \int_{(-\infty,b]} f\,d\mu \). Since \( \mu \) is
finite and atomless, \( L^{p} \subset L^{1} \) and the primitive \( F \) is
continuous.

**[B5]** The numerator is \( F(t+h) - F(t-h) \), a difference of continuous
functions on \( [0,1] \); divide by \( 2h \).

**[B6]** Hölder on the window gives
\( \int_{[t-h,t+h]}\lvert f\rvert\,d\mu \le \lVert f\rVert_{p}
(2h)^{1-1/p} \).

**[B7]** Hence \( \lvert f_h(t)\rvert \le (2h)^{-1/p}\lVert f\rVert_{p} \),
since \( (1-1/p)-1 = -1/p \); the bound holds for every real \( t \).
