# a99J07 publication telling

## Publication

**[B1]** Put \(M=\sup_{\lvert z\rvert\le1}\lvert p(z)\rvert\), which is
finite by compactness of the disk and continuity of \(p\).

**[B2]** We distinguish \(\lvert w\rvert\le1\) and
\(\lvert w\rvert>1\).

**[B3]** In the first case, \(\lvert p(w)\rvert\le M\) and
\(\max\{1,\lvert w\rvert\}^{N}=1\).

**[B4]** In the second case, let \(q\) be the polynomial obtained by
reversing the coefficients of \(p\) through degree \(N\), so that
\(q(z)=z^{N}p(1/z)\) for \(z\ne0\); this moves the exterior evaluation to
the disk while retaining the required degree factor.

**[B5]** For \(\lvert v\rvert=1\), the reflection identity gives
\(\lvert q(v)\rvert=\lvert p(1/v)\rvert\le M\).

**[B6]** Since \(q\) is a polynomial and \(u=1/w\) lies in the closed disk,
the boundary form of the maximum modulus principle supplies
\(\lvert v\rvert=1\) with \(\lvert q(u)\rvert\le\lvert q(v)\rvert\), and
hence \(\lvert q(1/w)\rvert\le M\).

**[B7]** Therefore \(p(w)=q(1/w)w^{N}\) gives
\(\lvert p(w)\rvert\le M\lvert w\rvert^{N}\), where
\(\max\{1,\lvert w\rvert\}=\lvert w\rvert\).

**[B8]** Combining the cases proves
\(\lvert p(w)\rvert\le M\max\{1,\lvert w\rvert\}^{N}\) for every
\(w\in\mathbb C\).
