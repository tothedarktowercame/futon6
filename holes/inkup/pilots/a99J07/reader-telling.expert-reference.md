# a99J07 reader-oriented telling

## Expert reference

**[B1]** Put \(M=\sup_{\lvert z\rvert\le1}\lvert p(z)\rvert\); compactness
of the closed disk gives \(\lvert p(z)\rvert\le M\) there.

**[B4]** For \(\lvert w\rvert>1\), let \(q\) be the polynomial obtained by
reversing the coefficients of \(p\) up to degree \(N\), so that
\(q(z)=z^{N}p(1/z)\) for \(z\ne0\).  This reflection moves the exterior
evaluation to \(1/w\) in the disk and preserves the factor \(w^{N}\) needed
to recover \(p(w)\).

**[B5]** On \(\lvert v\rvert=1\), the reflection identity gives
\(\lvert q(v)\rvert=\lvert p(1/v)\rvert\le M\).

**[B6]** The maximum modulus principle therefore gives
\(\lvert q(u)\rvert\le M\) for \(\lvert u\rvert\le1\).

**[B7]** Taking \(u=1/w\) yields
\(\lvert p(w)\rvert=\lvert q(1/w)\rvert\lvert w\rvert^{N}\le
M\lvert w\rvert^{N}\).  Together with the defining bound for
\(\lvert w\rvert\le1\), this proves
\(\lvert p(w)\rvert\le M\max\{1,\lvert w\rvert\}^{N}\) for all \(w\).
