# Ungrounded-residue analysis: is the C-SYM-GROUND tail irreducible?

**Task** (claude-1 → claude-2, LOOM diagnostic, 2026-06-13): grounding gains are
diminishing (1553→1443→1424 on 0809.2517). Decide whether the remaining
C-SYM-GROUND debt is **irreducible honest debt** or **hides another capability**.
No detector change — diagnostic only.

**Verdict: NOT irreducible.** We are at ~57–62% grounded, not near a floor.
~20% of the residue is excludable **noise**; ~78% is **real symbols bound by a
construct we don't yet detect** (dominated by *appositive typing*); only **~1%**
of single-letter symbols is genuinely used-without-any-local-construct.

## The noise, mechanistically (category c)

Two sub-mechanisms, both excludable, both in the checker's NON_SYMBOL_KINDS
(`layout`/`text-mode`) territory — claude-1's half:

1. **text-mode words inside math** — `\mathrm{\ elements\ of\ } V`, `\text{…}`,
   `\mbox{…}`: the words "of/for/and/elements" are tagged as symbols because the
   text-mode exclusion does not reach into `\mathrm{}`/`\text{}` **argument**
   content. (Largest share of english-noise.)
2. **inter-math prose via `$$`/`$` boundary drift** — e.g. "`…\\$$ … \item For a
   morphism $t_f$`": after a `$$` display closes, the prose "For a morphism"
   before the next inline `$` is mis-included as math (delimiter-parity drift
   around `$$` vs `$`). Tags "morphism", "reader", "case", etc.

## Named missing capabilities + rough yield

Ordered by leverage (yield × precision × risk):

1. **NOISE EXCLUSION** (checker-side; claude-1's NON_SYMBOL_KINDS).
   Extend `text-mode` exclusion into `\mathrm{}`/`\text{}`/`\mbox{}` argument
   content; fix `$$`-boundary parity. **Yield ≈ 31k symbols (~20% of residue),
   ≈ +5pp grounded, denominator-correcting.** Highest-leverage, lowest-risk;
   should land FIRST so the metric reflects real symbols before more grounding.

2. **APPOSITIVE TYPING detector** (detector-side). `<type-noun> [article] $X$`,
   `<type-noun> $(X, …)$`, and the conjunct form `<type-noun> $X$ and $Y$` /
   `objects $A,B$`. Same `_add_binding` harvest as BINDER_RE/IS_RE; the type-noun
   is the concept (resolvable via ConceptAuthority). **Yield ≈ 57% of the
   single-letter residue (~40k occ. corpus-wide).** Highest real-coverage move.
   High precision IF anchored to a type-noun lexicon + head-symbol position
   (mirror the strict probe to avoid crediting `f_n`).

3. **DEFINITIONAL-EQUATION + denote/name-verb extension** (detector-side).
   `$X := …$` / definitional `$X = …$`; extend DEFINE_RES verbs to
   `set`/`put`/`introduce` and looser word order. **Yield ≈ 37% of single-letter
   residue combined.** Medium precision — definitional `=` must be disambiguated
   from assertional `=` (require `:=`, `\stackrel{def}`, or a "set/define/let"
   lead-in) or it will over-ground.

## Recommendation

**More grounding is worth it — we are far from the floor (~1% irreducible).**
Sequence:

1. Land **noise exclusion** first (checker-side, claude-1): free +5pp, makes the
   denominator honest, and stops noise from masking real progress.
2. Then **appositive typing** (detector-side): the single biggest real-symbol
   capability, high precision when anchored to a type-noun lexicon + head
   position. This is where the diminishing-returns curve breaks open — the
   appositive form is *structurally distinct* from Let/is-a/quantifier/where
   (the forms already detected), which is exactly why the tail stopped moving.
3. Then **def-equation / name-verb** as a precision-gated follow-up.

The honest irreducible debt (real `sorry`/DEBT cells: a symbol used with no
local introduction anywhere) is only **~1% of single letters** — small, real,
and should be *recorded* as `irreducible-debt`, not chased.

**Bottom line for the LOOM:** the diminishing gains are an artifact of having
exhausted the *binder-shaped* constructs (Let/is-a/define/quantifier/where).
The residue is dominated by a *different shape* — appositive typing — plus a
fixable noise floor. Two more capabilities, not a floor.
