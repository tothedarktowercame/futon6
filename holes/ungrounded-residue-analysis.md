# Ungrounded-residue analysis: is the C-SYM-GROUND tail irreducible?

**Task** (claude-1 → claude-2, LOOM diagnostic, 2026-06-13): grounding gains are
diminishing (1553→1443→1424 on 0809.2517). Decide whether the remaining
C-SYM-GROUND debt is **irreducible honest debt** or **hides another capability**.
No detector change — diagnostic only.

**Verdict: NOT irreducible.** We are at ~57–62% grounded, not near a floor.
~20% of the residue is excludable **noise**; ~78% is **real symbols bound by a
construct we don't yet detect** (dominated by *appositive typing*); only **~1%**
of single-letter symbols is genuinely used-without-any-local-construct.

## (a)/(b)/(c) categorization of the *single-letter* residue (15-paper sample)

Attributed by the first introducing-construct signature found in the paper.
Strict appositive requires the letter to be the **head** symbol immediately
after a type-noun (so "function $f_n$" does NOT falsely credit `n`); it is a
**lower bound** (it misses conjuncts like "objects $K$ and $L$" for the second
symbol).

| introducing construct | % of single-letter occ. | category |
|---|---|---|
| **appositive typing** (`integer $n$`, `spaces $(V,q_V)$ and $(W,q_W)$`, `objects $K$ and $L$`, `object $X$ in …`) | **57%** | (b) |
| denote / name-verb (`set`/`put`/`introduce`/`denote` … `$X$`) | 21% | (b) |
| definitional equation (`$X := …$`, `$X = …$`) | 16% | (b) |
| appositive (loose signal only) | 2% | (b), weak |
| **irreducible — no local construct** | **~1%** | **(a)** |

## The noise, mechanistically (category c)

Two sub-mechanisms, both excludable, both in the checker's NON_SYMBOL_KINDS
(`layout`/`text-mode`) territory — claude-1's half:

1. **text-mode words inside math** — `\mathrm{\ elements\ of\ } V`, `\text{…}`,
   `\mbox{…}`: the words "of/for/and/elements" are tagged as symbols because the
   text-mode exclusion does not reach into `\mathrm{}`/`\text{}` **argument**
   content. (Largest share of english-noise.)
2. **inter-math prose via `$$`/`$` boundary drift** — e.g. "`…\\$$ … \item For a
   morphism $t_f$`": after a `$$` display closes, the prose "For a morphism"
   before the next inline `$` is mis-included as math (delimiter-parity drift
   around `$$` vs `$`). Tags "morphism", "reader", "case", etc.

## Named missing capabilities + rough yield

Ordered by leverage (yield × precision × risk):

1. **NOISE EXCLUSION** (checker-side; claude-1's NON_SYMBOL_KINDS).
   Extend `text-mode` exclusion into `\mathrm{}`/`\text{}`/`\mbox{}` argument
   content; fix `$$`-boundary parity. **Yield ≈ 31k symbols (~20% of residue),
   ≈ +5pp grounded, denominator-correcting.** Highest-leverage, lowest-risk;
   should land FIRST so the metric reflects real symbols before more grounding.

2. **APPOSITIVE TYPING detector** (detector-side). `<type-noun> [article] $X$`,
   `<type-noun> $(X, …)$`, and the conjunct form `<type-noun> $X$ and $Y$` /
   `objects $A,B$`. Same `_add_binding` harvest as BINDER_RE/IS_RE; the type-noun
   is the concept (resolvable via ConceptAuthority). **Yield ≈ 57% of the
   single-letter residue (~40k occ. corpus-wide).** Highest real-coverage move.
   High precision IF anchored to a type-noun lexicon + head-symbol position
   (mirror the strict probe to avoid crediting `f_n`).

3. **DEFINITIONAL-EQUATION + denote/name-verb extension** (detector-side).
   `$X := …$` / definitional `$X = …$`; extend DEFINE_RES verbs to
   `set`/`put`/`introduce` and looser word order. **Yield ≈ 37% of single-letter
   residue combined.** Medium precision — definitional `=` must be disambiguated
   from assertional `=` (require `:=`, `\stackrel{def}`, or a "set/define/let"
   lead-in) or it will over-ground.

## Recommendation

**More grounding is worth it — we are far from the floor (~1% irreducible).**
Sequence:

1. Land **noise exclusion** first (checker-side, claude-1): free +5pp, makes the
   denominator honest, and stops noise from masking real progress.
2. Then **appositive typing** (detector-side): the single biggest real-symbol
   capability, high precision when anchored to a type-noun lexicon + head
   position. This is where the diminishing-returns curve breaks open — the
   appositive form is *structurally distinct* from Let/is-a/quantifier/where
   (the forms already detected), which is exactly why the tail stopped moving.
3. Then **def-equation / name-verb** as a precision-gated follow-up.

The honest irreducible debt (real `sorry`/DEBT cells: a symbol used with no
local introduction anywhere) is only **~1% of single letters** — small, real,
and should be *recorded* as `irreducible-debt`, not chased.

**Bottom line for the LOOM:** the diminishing gains are an artifact of having
exhausted the *binder-shaped* constructs (Let/is-a/define/quantifier/where).
The residue is dominated by a *different shape* — appositive typing — plus a
fixable noise floor. Two more capabilities, not a floor.

Two structural facts drive the categorization:

- **Index/dummy letters dominate the single-letter bucket**: `i,j,k,l,m,n,p,q,
  r,s,t,d` = **53,429 (33% of all single letters)**. A summation/subscript index
  (`\sum_i`, `x_i`) is a *bound dummy* — it has no introducing construct to
  detect, and grounding it to a "type" is not meaningful. This is honest floor,
  not a missed capability.
- **The multichar bucket is mostly boundary-drift prose**, not symbols: its top
  tokens are `the×6543, of×3983, is×2736, and×2410, to, in, that, for, we, by,
  be, with, are, an, category…` — English words mis-tagged as symbols where a
  `$$`-display closes and inter-formula prose runs into the next `$`. Plus a tail
  of GrCalc display-macro fragments (`rr, dr, dd, dl, Fo, ur`) and a few real
  compound symbols (`ij, id, op`). So a large slice of "multichar-alpha" + all of
  "english-noise" is the **same $$/$ boundary-parity noise** — together ≈ 75–80k.

## (a)/(b)/(c) verdict

- **(c) Residual NOISE — ≈ 24% of the ungrounded (≈ 75–80k).** Boundary-drift
  English prose (the/of/is/and/that/for…) mis-tagged via `$$`/`$` parity drift,
  plus GrCalc display fragments. This is the **already-identified, already-scoped
  `$$/$` boundary-parity ROOT fix** (claude-1's checker-half + claude-4's
  wf-error car) — a DENOMINATOR correction, **not a new grounding lever**.
  Excluding it lifts pooled grounded **66.4% → ~72–73%** with zero new detection.
- **(a) IRREDUCIBLE / DUMMY — the bulk of the single-letter core.** Index/dummy
  variables (53k+) and generic variables (`x,y,z,a,b,c,u,v,g…`) used throughout
  with no local typing introduction. No construct introduces a free index — this
  is honest `irreducible-debt`, the achievable floor.
- **(b) NEXT detectable construct — appositive LEXICON EXTENSION (incremental).**
  The one genuine remaining grounding lever. Sample (24 papers): residue head-
  letters preceded by `<determiner> … <noun> $X$` where the noun is NOT in the
  current `TYPE_NOUNS` lexicon = **754 gap-occurrences** vs **521 in-lexicon
  misses**. Top gap type-nouns: `point, element, pair, vector, sequence, family,
  lattice, bundle, connection, inclusion, cospan, cover, product, triple`. But:
  (i) yield is **far smaller** than the original appositive lever (−601); (ii)
  ~40% of "gap nouns" are false matches (`and, that, for, unique, principal` —
  not type-nouns); (iii) several real candidates are precision-risky (`form`,
  `relation`, `theory` often DON'T type a single symbol: "a relation between
  $x$ and $y$", "in the form of $X$"). The 521 in-lexicon misses are
  detector-tightening (determiner/adjacency variants), not a new construct.

## Recommendation: near the achievable ceiling; one small lever left

We have largely exhausted construct-based grounding. The two biggest residue
chunks are **not** new-lever territory:

1. **Residual boundary-drift noise (~24%)** → the already-scoped `$$/$` parity
   root fix. Do this first: it is a denominator correction worth **~+6pp**
   (66.4% → ~72–73%) and it also kills claude-4's wf-error family (one root).
2. **Index/dummy/generic variables** → irreducible honest debt; record as
   `irreducible-debt`, do not chase.

The ONE worthwhile remaining grounding lever is **a conservative appositive
lexicon extension** — add only high-confidence, unambiguous type-nouns
(`point, element, pair, vector, sequence, family, bundle, lattice`) under the
existing determiner + head-position gate; **exclude** the ambiguous ones
(`form, relation, theory, number`). Estimated yield: a **few percentage points**
of numerator grounding, not another −601. Optionally recover the 521 in-lexicon
misses via a small determiner/adjacency loosening (precision-gated).

**Bottom line:** after (1) the pending boundary-parity denominator fix and (2)
one conservative lexicon extension, the corpus reaches its structural ceiling —
roughly **mid-to-high 70s % grounded** — and the remainder is honest irreducible
debt (bound indices, dummy/generic variables with no introduction). There is no
third big lever; the curve has flattened by design, not by oversight.
